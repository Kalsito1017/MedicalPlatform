# Demo staff portraits

These are **synthetic, demo-only profile images** for the three canonical seeded
staff accounts. They are not photographs of real staff at this deployment and
must not be presented as real clinician identities.

- Source: [This Person Does Not Exist](https://thispersondoesnotexist.com/)
  (StyleGAN2, Karras et al.), downloaded 2026-11-11. The built-in workspace
  image generator was unavailable (insufficient balance) when these were added.
- Processing: cropped, resized to 128×128 JPEG, and stripped of metadata.
- Files: `admin-1.jpg`, `doctor-1.jpg`, `nurse-1.jpg`.
- The site does not publish separate per-image licensing terms. These files are
  demo placeholders; replace them with approved, licensed staff photos (or a
  proper identity provider) before any real deployment.
- Do not use these images for identity verification, biometric matching, or to
  imply that a synthetic account belongs to a real person.
