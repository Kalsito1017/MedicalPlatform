# Demo doctor portraits

These four portraits are **demo-only placeholder profile images** used for the
synthetic doctor accounts in this application. They are downloaded from
[randomuser.me](https://randomuser.me/) (whose placeholder photos are drawn from
the authorized UI Faces collection).

- They are not photographs of real staff at this deployment.
- They must not be presented as real clinician identities.
- Replace them with approved, licensed staff photos (or a proper identity
  provider) before any real deployment.
- Files: `doctor-1.jpg` … `doctor-4.jpg`, 128×128 JPEG.
- The three canonical seeded staff accounts use separate synthetic portraits in
  `../staff/` (see `../staff/README.md`).
