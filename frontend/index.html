Clinical AI Platform — synthetic data demonstration

A demonstration of a role-aware clinical AI platform. Sign in to search fictional patients, inspect synthetic lab results and prescriptions, and see how role-based access control shapes the interface.

What this demo is

  • Every patient, encounter, lab, and prescription is fictional synthetic data.
  • Access is enforced server-side by role: doctor, nurse, clinical administrator, technical administrator.
  • Security-relevant actions are audited on the backend.

What this demo is not

  • Not clinically validated, not a medical device, and not for real patient care.
  • Not a place to enter real personal or health information — demo accounts are shared and public.
  • AI-generated answers (where enabled) are informational only and are labelled as AI-generated.
