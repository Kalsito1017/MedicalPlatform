3. Information the platform processes

Account and authentication information

Staff accounts may include a display name, email address, role assignment, account status, and a password hash. Authentication records may include session metadata such as IP address, user agent, timestamps, session expiry and revocation state, CSRF tokens, login outcomes, and security audit events.

Clinical and operational information

In the current deployment, patient demographics, encounters, clinical notes, laboratory results, prescriptions, appointments, messages, attachments, and AI conversation history are synthetic. In a real deployment, these categories could contain personal and health data and would require the safeguards, notices, and lawful basis described by the controller.

Voice and AI information

Voice recordings are processed transiently for transcription and are not intentionally persisted by the application. The transcript is returned to the user and may be stored in AI conversation history if it is submitted to the assistant. When the AI assistant is enabled, the question and retrieved record data may be sent to the configured external model provider. Provider terms, retention, training, residency, and subprocessors must be verified before real data is used.

Technical information

The application may process request identifiers, operational and error logs, health-check results, and security audit events. The application does not use advertising trackers, session recording, keystroke capture, or third-party product analytics.

4. Why information is processed

  • Provide, operate, and secure the platform.
  • Authenticate users and enforce role-based access.
  • Maintain audit trails and investigate security events.
  • Operate, debug, and improve reliability.
  • Provide the optional AI assistant and voice transcription.
  • Respond to support and privacy requests through verified channels.
  • Meet legal obligations that apply to the deployment.

The platform does not sell personal information and does not use it for advertising.

9. Retention

The current implementation has the following demo-scope behavior. Production retention must be confirmed by the controller and legal counsel.

  • Sessions: 8 hours by default; expired or revoked sessions are rejected. Session rows may remain until cleanup.
  • Audit events: retained for the lifetime of the demo database; no automated deletion or erasure job is implemented.
  • Operational logs: best-effort pruning after 7 days.
  • AI conversation history: retained until the user deletes it or the demo data is reset.
  • Idempotency records: retained for 24 hours.
  • Messages and attachments: retained with the message and the demo database.
  • Voice audio and transcripts: audio is not intentionally persisted; the transcript is returned to the user and may be stored in AI history if submitted.
  • Backups: only if the deployment owner configures them; see the deployment documentation.

[PRODUCTION RETENTION SCHEDULE — TO BE CONFIRMED]

10. Security

The application uses argon2id password hashing, server-side sessions with hashed tokens, role-based access control, CSRF protection, security headers, and audit events. Production use requires TLS, network isolation, least-privilege database roles, monitoring, backup and restore procedures, and independent security review. No claim of encryption at rest, certification, or complete security is made unless it has been independently verified.
